
    📌 Text Elements  
    -----------------
        Used to structure text content.

            🔹 <h1> to <h6>	    Headings
            🔹 <p>	            Paragraph
            🔹 <br>	            Line break
            🔹 <hr>	            Horizontal line
            🔹 <div>            Division
            🔹 <blockquote>     block + quote
            🔹 <pre>            Preformatted text.
